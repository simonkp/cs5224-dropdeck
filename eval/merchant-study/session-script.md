# Merchant session script (45 min)

Two people: a facilitator (talks) and a note-taker (times tasks, records hesitations and errors).
Use the `staging` site with a fresh merchant account created for the participant.

## 1. Consent (3 min)

> This session is for a CS5224 course project at NUS. We're testing the tool, not you. We'll take notes and
> time the tasks; with your OK we'll record the screen, not your face. Your name won't appear in our report,
> you can stop at any time, and we delete the notes and recordings after the module ends. Is that OK?

Record: verbal consent yes / no, recording yes / no.

## 2. Warm-up: current practice (7 min)

1. Walk me through your last drop, from announcing it to handing over the goods.
2. Where do you take orders today (form, DMs, storefront)? How do you know when you've sold out?
3. Has overselling or a crashed page ever happened? What did it cost you (time, refunds, reputation)?
4. Roughly how many people watch a drop, and how many units do you sell?

## 3. Tasks (20 min, think aloud, no help unless stuck for 2 min)

For each task note: start/end time, completed unaided (Y/N), errors, places they hesitated, quotes.

| # | Task (read aloud) | Success means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | "Set up your shop: name, logo or colour, and your PayNow number." | Profile saved with PayNow details |
| T2 | "Create a drop for this Saturday: two items, 30 of one and 20 of the other, opening at 9am, limit 2 per buyer. Then schedule it and copy the link." | Drop scheduled, link copied |
| T3 | "The drop has just finished. Find who still hasn't paid, mark two orders as paid, and download the order list." | Two orders marked paid, CSV downloaded |

Before T3, the note-taker runs a synthetic drop against the participant's drop so real-looking orders exist.

## 4. SUS questionnaire (5 min)

Hand over `sus.md` (or the online form). No discussion while they fill it in.

## 5. Interview (8 min)

1. What, if anything, would stop you using this for your next drop?
2. What was the most confusing moment just now?
3. Pricing: Starter is S$29 a month for up to 2 drops; Growth is S$79 a month including 5,000 buyer
   sessions; a one-off drop is S$19. Which fits you, and would you pay it? What would feel fair?
4. Buyers confirm their order with an email code. Would your buyers accept that? Would you want it off?
5. Anything you expected to see that wasn't there?

## 6. Close (2 min)

Thank them; offer a copy of the findings. Note-taker writes up within 24 hours in `notes/<pseudonym>.md`.
