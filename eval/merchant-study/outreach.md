# Outreach

## Who to look for

Home bakers, roasters, small streetwear or sneaker resellers, craft makers, and workshop organisers who
announce limited releases at a set time on Instagram or Telegram. Good signs: "drop", "restock at 9pm",
"preorders open Sat", a Google Form in the bio, or posts apologising for overselling.

Sources: team members' own networks first (warm introductions convert best), then Instagram hashtags
(#sghomebaker, #sgsmallbusiness, #sgstreetwear), Telegram channels, and STePS visitors.

## First message (DM)

> Hi [name], I'm [your name], a Master's student at NUS. Our team is building a small tool for sellers who do
> timed drops like your [Saturday bakes / restocks]: a countdown page with a fair queue that stops overselling
> and gives you a clean order list afterwards.
>
> Would you be open to a 45-minute session (online or at your place, around 26 Oct to 8 Nov) where you try it
> on a pretend drop and tell us what you think? No cost, no sales pitch, and nothing is connected to your
> real customers or payments. Happy to share what we find.

## Follow-up (after 4–5 days, once only)

> Hi [name], just following up on my note about the drop tool. If the timing's bad, even a 15-minute call
> about how you handle drops today would help us a lot. Thanks either way!

## If they say yes

Confirm date, format (online / in person), and that they are 18 or older. Send the consent note from
`session-script.md` the day before. Log everything in `tracker.csv`.
