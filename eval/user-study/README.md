# User study results

Responses to the two surveys used in §4.5 of the final report. Both surveys closed on 11 Nov 2026 and were
exported from Google Sheets that day.

| File | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [`questions.md`](questions.md) | The questions asked in both surveys |
| [`buyer-survey.csv`](buyer-survey.csv) | Buyer trial and survey: 17 responses |
| [`seller-survey.csv`](seller-survey.csv) | Seller survey: 9 responses |

## How the data was prepared

- Respondents were recruited from people known to the team, and all gave consent for anonymous use in the report.
- Timestamps are reduced to the date. Respondents are numbered B01–B17 and S01–S09 in the order they replied.
- Seller answers to "What products do you sell?" are replaced by a general category, so no shop can be identified.
- One buyer response (10 Nov) has a timestamp in the consent field instead of "Yes". Because consent is not
  recorded, it is left out. The buyer sheet therefore had 18 rows and this file has 17.
- The consent column is dropped, since every remaining row answered "Yes". All other answers, including
  typos in free text, are unchanged.

## Summary

These samples are small and probably favourable, so the counts describe these respondents only.

**Buyers (17)**

- Joining: 14 joined the waiting room without issues and 3 could not join. One of the 3 (B06) also reported
  completing checkout, which does not match their answer to Q7.
- Purchase: 13 completed checkout, 2 were offered an item but did not complete checkout, and 2 did not reach
  the queue.
- Fairness (Q11): 13 rated 5, 2 rated 4, 2 rated 3.
- Ease of navigation (Q15): 15 rated 5, 2 rated 4.
- Preference for random allocation after trying DropDeck (Q13): 11 rated 5, 2 rated 4, 3 rated 3, 1 rated 2.
  Before trying it (Q6), 6 preferred random allocation, 3 first-come, 4 had no preference and 4 were not sure.
- Would buy through DropDeck (Q19): 9 definitely, 7 probably, 1 not sure.
- Suggestions: integrated payment (Apple Pay or a PayNow QR code), adding the drop to a calendar, and
  filtering and sorting live drops.

**Sellers (9)**

- 8 viewed the prototype; 1 had not yet. None used it hands-on.
- Orders a month: 0–10 (3), 11–30 (1), 31–50 (3), 51–100 (1), 101–300 (1).
- Problems in the past year: 3 had accepted more orders than stock, 5 had difficulty tracking payments,
  5 had too many messages or manual work, and 2 reported no significant problems.
- Usefulness (1–5): 5, 5, 5, 4, 4, 3, 3, 2, 1.
- Plan they would choose: Event (3), Starter (2), Growth (2), not at these prices (2).
