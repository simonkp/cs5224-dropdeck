# Survey questions

Question wording is copied from the column headers of the two Google Forms response sheets. The answer options
listed are the ones that appear in the responses. Options nobody chose are not recorded here, so the lists may
be incomplete. Rating questions use a 1–5 scale; the wording of the scale's end points was not recorded.
